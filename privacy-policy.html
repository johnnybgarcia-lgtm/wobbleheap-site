Privacy Policy — Wobbleheap

Last updated: July 23, 2026
Short version: Wobbleheap collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no internet connection. Nothing you or your child does in the app ever leaves the device.

Information we collect

None. Wobbleheap does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or any account of any kind.

Analytics and advertising

Wobbleheap contains no analytics software, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind. No usage data, device identifiers, or crash reports are gathered by us.

Network access

Wobbleheap makes no network requests. All artwork, sound, fonts and code are contained inside the app itself. The game works completely offline, including in airplane mode.

Data stored on your device

The app saves one thing locally on your own device: your highest score, so it can be shown the next time you play. It never leaves the device, is not linked to any identity, and is deleted permanently if you delete the app.

Children's privacy

Wobbleheap is designed to be suitable for children. Because the app collects no data whatsoever and has no network access, no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, no external links and no purchases, there is no personal information from children (or anyone else) for us to collect, use, or disclose. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting nothing at all.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version adds a feature that requires data — we will update this page and the "last updated" date above, and describe the change plainly.
