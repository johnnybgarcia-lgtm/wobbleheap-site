Common questions

The toppings are hard to tell apart.

Tap 123 at the top of the board to switch every tile to numbers. Your choice is remembered.

I'm stuck on a puzzle.

Press Teach. It names the next logical move and explains why it works. Reading the explanation is free — you only spend a hint if you ask it to place the answer for you.

How do I change the difficulty?

Options → Difficulty of the journey. It applies to the next board you start, not the one you're on. Free play and the daily bowl set their own.

Are the puzzles the same for everyone?

Only the daily bowl, which is the same board worldwide and changes at midnight. Every other puzzle is generated fresh on your device and checked to have exactly one solution.

I lost my progress.

Progress is stored on the device and isn't linked to an account, so it doesn't move between phones and is cleared if you delete the app. There's no way to recover it — sorry.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device — on a plane, underground, anywhere.

Privacy

Ramen Sudoku collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no data leaves your device. Your puzzle progress and settings are stored locally on your phone and are deleted along with the app.

There are no third-party services, SDKs or trackers in the app. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is ever collected.
